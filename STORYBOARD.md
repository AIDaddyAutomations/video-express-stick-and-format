# Every Ring, a Little More Relief

**Format:** 60-second landscape video (16:9)  
**Audience:** Small-business owners who struggle to answer every incoming call  
**Voice:** One consistent voice throughout. The on-camera AI Daddy spokesperson speaks the opening; that same selected voice continues as narration over every stickman scene. Alexis is represented visually and is not given a second audible voice.  
**Visual structure:** Photorealistic spokesperson introduction, followed by story-matched 2D stickman scenes. No anime or watercolor.  
**Call to action:** Self-guided demo with Alexis at **773-492-8331**.

## Story premise

Jordan runs a neighborhood repair shop. A rush of calls arrives while Jordan is already helping a customer. The interruptions make the workday feel impossible. The AI Daddy spokesperson introduces Alexis, the Voice AI Front Desk, and explains how it can answer calls and support lead capture, appointment booking, and follow-up. Jordan returns attention to the customer in front of them, while callers still have a next step.

## Script and scene plan

### 1. Spokesperson on camera — 0:00–0:10

**Spoken:** “When every call feels urgent, running your business can start to feel like answering phones all day instead of helping customers.”

**Visual:** Photorealistic AI Daddy spokesperson in a warm, modern small-business setting, medium shot, speaking directly to camera with a calm, empathetic delivery. Keep the shot simple and steady. This is the first segment of the single voice used throughout.

### 2. Spokesperson on camera — 0:10–0:20

**Spoken:** “AI Daddy Automations can help. Meet Alexis, your Voice AI Front Desk—ready to answer calls and help your team keep leads and appointments moving.”

**Visual:** Continue with the same photorealistic spokesperson, face, wardrobe, lighting, and framing. A restrained gesture introduces an unobtrusive phone or front-desk visual. Do not show a second speaking character. The selected voice stays exactly the same.

### 3. Stickman scene — 0:20–0:30

**Spoken (same voice, now voiceover):** “Jordan owns a neighborhood repair shop. On Monday morning, calls arrive back-to-back while one customer is already waiting at the counter.”

**Visual prompt:** Landscape 16:9, clean hand-drawn 2D stickman animation with crisp black contours, white circular heads, expressive simple faces, two clear arms and legs per person, flat cel colors, and layered illustrated depth. Jordan, a small-business owner in a teal work shirt and dark apron, stands behind the counter helping one customer. A phone lights up beside a short stack of work orders; a second caller is suggested by a simple phone icon or split-screen silhouette. Show Jordan glancing toward the ringing phone while still facing the customer. One fixed repair-shop layout: counter foreground, tool wall behind Jordan, front window at screen right. Gentle, readable movement. No realistic humans, anime, watercolor, generated lettering, subtitles, or dialogue.

### 4. Stickman scene — 0:30–0:40

**Spoken (same voice):** “Alexis can answer incoming calls, respond to common questions, and capture what each caller needs, giving the business a clearer next step.”

**Visual prompt:** Continue the same 2D stickman style and repair-shop layout. Show a simple phone waveform transforming into an organized call card beside Jordan’s counter; use icons rather than small text. Jordan stays engaged with the customer while the call card receives a name and request symbol. Keep the phone, work orders, counter, and tool wall in stable positions. Calm, helpful mood, one clear action, no second audible voice.

### 5. Stickman scene — 0:40–0:50

**Spoken (same voice):** “When configured for the business, Alexis can help with appointment booking, missed-call text-back, and consistent lead follow-up.”

**Visual prompt:** Show the same Jordan and repair-shop counter. A calendar icon gains one highlighted appointment slot; a missed-call icon receives a small message bubble; a lead card moves into a tidy follow-up column. Keep the three visual actions clean and sequential, with readable icons and no generated words. All people remain 2D stickmen in their established outfits. No interface clutter or new human characters.

### 6. Stickman closing and end card — 0:50–1:00

**Spoken (same voice):** “Jordan can focus on the customer in front of them, with fewer calls left without a next step. Try the self-guided demo: call Alexis at 773-492-8331.”

**Visual prompt:** Return to the stable shop layout. Jordan smiles and hands the repaired item to the customer; the phone rests nearby with a neat row of call cards and one appointment icon. Ease into a clean end card with the exact, large, legible text: “Try the self-guided demo with Alexis” and “773-492-8331”. Add “AI Daddy Automations” as the brand line. Keep the end card on screen long enough to read. Use a designed text overlay in editing rather than asking the image model to render the phone number. No additional voices.

## Production notes

- Use the same selected system voice for all six spoken segments. If VideoExpress requires separate audio generations, select the same voice each time and keep the voice setting unchanged.
- The first two segments are on-camera spokesperson speech; the last four use that voice as narration over stickman animation. Keep clip audio clean and avoid adding an Alexis voice.
- Keep the spokesperson photorealistic only in the opening. Every person in animated story scenes, including background people, is a 2D stickman.
- Use the same Jordan design, teal shirt, dark apron, repair-shop geography, and left-to-right action throughout scenes 3–6.
- Keep each claim conditional where setup matters. The story demonstrates listed service capabilities without promising a particular business outcome.
- Use the phone number exactly as supplied: **773-492-8331**. The CTA invites viewers to try the self-guided demo; do not imply anyone has already called.
